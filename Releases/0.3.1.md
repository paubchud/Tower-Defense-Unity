# 0.3.1 - Experimental land and economy playtest

Paired Windows/macOS development test, not a stable or production release. Download the ZIP for your platform under Assets, extract everything, and use matching 0.3.1 with your friend. Guest EOS and explicit Steam App ID 480 tests remain separate; no cross-play, account sign-in requirement for guests, paid billing or persistent progression is introduced.

## Added

- Two purchasable plots and two own-side stone nodes per player, with authored ownership.
- **B** opens land/tower selection, purchase, build, partial refunds and one hero-growth purchase. The build catalog comes from your class's technology group.
- Select pickaxe, approach a labeled own-side rock, press **E** and stay nearby for two seconds: 5 stone. Node reserves, stone capacity and depleted-node recovery are enforced by the host.
- Built towers defend alongside the starter tower. Gold-funded growth adds 25 maximum HP once per match, without immediately healing; the next respawn fills it.
- Ghosts can manage purchases/builds/sales/growth, but never mine; death/tool/range changes cancel harvesting. Match end stops collection, and rematch resets temporary growth/assets/reserves.
- Shared round/identity/command-sequence admission, atomic costs/refunds, owner-only reserves/materials/progress, bounded changed-state replication and host-only diagnostic transaction journal.

Defaults are editable, not final balance: start 50 gold; land 25 gold/refund 12; tower 20 stone/refund 10; 100 stone capacity; 50 per node, recovery 20 seconds after depletion. Make inventory room before selling a tower; sell towers before land. **U** upgrades sent troops with XP, **T** sends, left mouse attacks with your weapon, WASD moves, right-drag orbits, and wheel selects hotbar slots.
